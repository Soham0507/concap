01 The problem

You spent $80,000 on a booth. You'll forget most of what got said by Tuesday.

Three days on the floor. 400 conversations. A pile of business cards, a half-filled scanner app, and a Slack thread full of "remember to follow up with the guy from the Series B fintech."

By the time you sit down to log leads, you remember names. Maybe titles. Almost never the specifics — what they're trying to solve, who their current vendor is, what they said about budget timing, the casual mention that they're switching ERPs in Q2.

That context is the lead. And it's gone.

  • Badge scanners capture identity. Not context. You leave with a name and zero memory of why they mattered.
  • End-of-day CRM entry loses 60-80% of detail. You're tired. You're hungry. You're guessing.
  • Phone voice memos require you to step away from the booth. You won't. And nobody transcribes them.

With Concap
  • Captures the conversation as it actually happens. The exact words. The exact context.

  • One device per rep. Scales linearly with your team. No retraining. No weird booth dynamics.
