# Consent & Privacy Profile Score

## Objective

Create a Consent & Privacy Profile that measures and rewards users who consistently follow ethical recording practices by obtaining consent before recording conversations.

The goal is to build trust, encourage responsible AI usage, and recognize users who prioritize privacy and transparency.

## User Story

As a platform user, I want my privacy-conscious behavior to be recognized and scored so that I can demonstrate my commitment to ethical communication and trust-building.

## Feature Overview

The system should analyze recorded conversations and determine whether the user explicitly requested consent before recording or processing the conversation.

Based on this behavior, the system will calculate a Consent & Privacy Score that is displayed on the user's profile.

## Metrics

### Consent Request Detection

The AI should identify phrases such as:

* "Is it okay if I record this conversation?"
* "Do I have your permission to record?"
* "May I record this call for note-taking purposes?"
* "This conversation may be recorded. Do you consent?"

The system should classify:

* Consent Requested
* Consent Confirmed
* Consent Not Requested
* Consent Declined

### Privacy Score Calculation

Example Formula:

Privacy Score =

(Conversations with Consent Requested ÷ Total Recorded Conversations) × 100

Example:

* Total Conversations: 100
* Conversations with Consent Request: 92

Privacy Score: 92%

## Profile Badges

### Privacy Champion

Awarded when:

* Privacy Score ≥ 90%
* Minimum 20 recorded conversations

Description:
Consistently requests consent before recording conversations.

### Community Trust Builder

Awarded when:

* Privacy Score ≥ 80%
* Positive consent behavior maintained over time

Description:
Promotes transparency and trust in communication.

### Ethical Communicator

Awarded when:

* Privacy Score ≥ 70%

Description:
Demonstrates responsible use of AI and recording tools.

## AI Analysis

For each conversation, AI should determine:

* Was consent requested?
* Was consent granted?
* Was recording initiated before consent?
* Were privacy expectations explained?

Store these results for auditing and reporting.

## Insights Dashboard

Display trends such as:

* Weekly Consent Rate
* Monthly Consent Rate
* Consent Compliance Percentage
* Trust Score Growth

## Notifications

Encourage positive behavior:

Examples:

* "Great job! You requested consent in 95% of your conversations this month."
* "Your Privacy Champion badge has been earned."
* "Requesting consent before recording can improve your trust score."

## Acceptance Criteria

* AI detects consent-related language from transcripts.
* Privacy Score is automatically calculated.
* Profile displays score and badges.
* Historical consent analytics are available.
* Users receive recognition for responsible behavior.
* Score updates after every processed conversation.
