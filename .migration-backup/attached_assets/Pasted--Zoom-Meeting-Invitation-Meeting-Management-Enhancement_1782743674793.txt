## Zoom Meeting Invitation & Meeting Management Enhancement

Enhance the Zoom integration by implementing a complete meeting invitation and management workflow similar to the native Zoom experience.

### 1. Meeting Invitations

* Add an **Invite Participants** option while creating or editing a Zoom meeting.
* Allow users to enter one or multiple email addresses.
* Automatically send a Zoom meeting invitation email to all invited participants once the meeting is scheduled.
* The invitation email should include:

  * Meeting Title
  * Date & Time
  * Time Zone
  * Zoom Meeting Link
  * Meeting ID
  * Passcode (if applicable)
  * Organizer Name
  * "Join Meeting" button/link
* Allow users to resend invitations at any time.

### 2. Meeting Card UI

Each scheduled meeting card should display:

* Meeting Title
* Meeting Status (Scheduled, Ongoing, Completed, Cancelled)
* Date & Time
* Duration
* Number of Attendees
* Meeting ID (optional)
* Organizer Name (optional)

### 3. Action Buttons

Include the following fully functional action buttons on every meeting card:

* **Join** – Opens the Zoom meeting directly.
* **Copy Link** – Copies the meeting URL to the clipboard.
* **Invite** – Opens a dialog to invite additional participants and sends invitation emails.
* **View Participants** – Displays all invited participants and their invitation status.
* **Edit** – Modify meeting details and notify participants of any updates.
* **Delete** – Cancel the meeting after confirmation and optionally notify all participants.
* **Fetch Transcript** – Retrieve the meeting transcript after the meeting has ended (if available).
* **Paste** – Paste a copied Zoom meeting link or details when required.
* **Refresh** – Synchronize and fetch the latest meeting status from Zoom.
* **Disconnect** – Disconnect the connected Zoom account.

### 4. Additional Functionality

* Display attendee count on the meeting card.
* Show invitation status for each participant (Pending, Accepted, Declined, No Response).
* Automatically update meeting status based on Zoom.
* Disable **Join** until the meeting start time (or follow Zoom's behavior).
* Show loading indicators during API calls.
* Display success and error toast messages for all actions.
* Confirm before deleting or disconnecting.
* Refresh the meeting list automatically after creating, editing, deleting, or inviting participants.

### 5. User Experience

* Match the overall workflow and user experience of Zoom as closely as possible.
* Ensure all buttons are fully functional with proper backend integration and error handling.
* Maintain a clean, responsive, and intuitive UI consistent with the existing meeting management interface.
